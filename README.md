## Why GoldList?

Great resources are scattered across hundreds of lists, threads and bookmarks you'll never open again. Giant "awesome" lists have **thousands** of links, and nobody reads them.

GoldList keeps **only the best** in each category:

- 🏆 **Curated, not collected:** every entry has to earn its place, and each category stays short.
- 🧭 **Everything in one place:** jobs, career, scholarships, learning, dev tools, AI, design, productivity and privacy.
- ✅ **Always alive:** every link is checked automatically each week.
- 🏷️ **Clear labels:** you know at a glance what's open source, fully free, or a free tier.

**Legend:** 🔓 Open source · 🆓 Free · 💳 Free tier · ⭐ Gold pick (best in category)
